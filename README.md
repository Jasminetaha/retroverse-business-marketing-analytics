# Retroverse — Business & Marketing Analytics

> Turning historical e-commerce data into customer, sales,
> and marketing decisions.

**Python • Pandas • Power BI • Shopify • Predictive Analytics**

## Project Overview

Retroverse is an end-to-end business and marketing analytics
project built using more than three years of historical Shopify data.

The project analyzes sales, customer behavior, products, retention,
and acquisition channels, then combines customer segmentation,
sales forecasting, and marketing scenario modeling to support
data-driven growth decisions.

### Business Objective

The analysis was designed to answer:

- Who are Retroverse's highest-value customers?
- What drives sales and repeat purchasing?
- Which products and acquisition channels perform best?
- What are future baseline sales expected to look like?
- How should a 30,000 EGP marketing budget be allocated?

## Key Analysis Areas

### Sales Analytics
Revenue, orders, AOV, growth trends and sales performance.

### Customer Intelligence
RFM segmentation, repeat purchasing, retention and customer value.

### Product Analytics
Product performance, demand and profitability analysis.

### Marketing Analytics
Acquisition channels, traffic quality and conversion behavior.

### Predictive Analytics
Customer behavior modeling and baseline sales forecasting.

### Marketing Optimization
Conservative, expected and optimistic scenarios were used to
evaluate potential allocations of a 30,000 EGP marketing budget,
including first-time Meta advertising.

## Key Findings

* Analyzed 3+ years of Shopify data, covering 179 unique orders, 162 customers, product performance, acquisition sources, and customer behavior.
* RFM segmentation revealed meaningful differences in customer value and engagement, supporting targeted retention and repeat-purchase strategies rather than uniform marketing.
* Retroverse had not historically run Meta Ads, so paid social was modeled as a new acquisition opportunity rather than using unsupported historical ROAS assumptions.
* Developed conservative, expected, and optimistic marketing scenarios to evaluate how a 30,000 EGP budget could be deployed across acquisition, retargeting, and retention.
* Built a baseline sales forecast so incremental marketing-driven growth could be distinguished from sales expected without additional advertising.
* Identified important data-quality limitations—including duplicate order line items, cancelled/voided transactions, and incomplete historical product costs—and accounted for them before generating business recommendations.
